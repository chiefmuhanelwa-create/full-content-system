R50,000. March 2021. That was my best month.

Two months later: R8,000. Same effort. Same posting schedule. Same audience. Different algorithm.

R50,000. R8,000. Sixty days apart.

March 2021 → May 2021.

March 2021: R50,000 in one month. First time in my life I'd crossed that mark. I was convinced I had cracked it. I started planning what to do with "this level of income" every month.

Facebook changed their algorithm. Video content got deprioritised. My reach dropped. Brand deals pulled back. AdSense earnings collapsed.

May 2021: R8,000. That's it. R8,000. I hadn't changed anything. The platform had.

That's the platform trap. And it doesn't just happen to small creators. It happened to me at 3 million cross-platform followers. It will happen to anyone who builds a business entirely on someone else's land.

Google AdSense. Meta. TikTok Creator Fund. YouTube. They are distribution channels — not income sources you own. Every algorithm update is a rent increase you didn't agree to.

The Savanna Cider retainer (R25,000/month for four months) saved me that year. One direct brand relationship with a contract. That was mine. The algorithm couldn't touch it.

"You don't own your Instagram following. You rent it. And the landlord — Meta — can change the terms anytime. The email list is the one asset the platform can't repossess."

What you can own.

An email list. A WhatsApp broadcast group. A digital product that sells through your own checkout. These are assets you control. No algorithm decides how many of your people see your content.

The DARES framework: Digital / Automated / Recurring / Evergreen / Scalable. Build income streams with these five qualities. If any stream can be wiped out by a single platform decision — it doesn't qualify as DARES. It's a dependency.

The goal isn't to leave social media. The goal is to stop letting it be your only foundation.
